# Surface: index.html (whole app)

Mode: Operate. Audience: one German student, 12+, practising irregular verbs on an iPad at home. Short ten-question sessions, verb list as reference and read-aloud run. Constraints: single file, vanilla JS, data block untouched, light and dark selectable in-app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The trainer is the student's own exercise book: fine squared paper, fineliner-black type, and neon highlighter as the only colour. It refuses the gamified category default of rounded colourful cards, emoji tiles and mascots.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white squared paper (blue-grey grid), ink navy #1d2433 for type, rules and the primary button; highlighter yellow, green and pink as state colour only (yellow = focus/current/selected, green = right/sicher, pink = wrong/falsch). Ruled list rows with 2px top rule, white fields with ink borders, Bricolage Grotesque self-hosted. Dark: the same notebook under a desk lamp at night, navy paper, light ink, markers keep dark ink on top.

STORY: She opens the app, sees at once how many verbs are sicher and which are shaky, picks a drill from the ruled list, answers one question per screen, and sees the right form highlighted like in her own notes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Big two-line title with "Trainer" highlighted; the progress count large at left with a 70-cell strip of the verbs coloured by level beneath; then the selection row and the ruled list of drills, primary rows at full width.

FORM: Heft & Textmarker, my top-ranked grounded candidate (1 of 7), chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK. seed:cead32e15753c992. Signature move: the highlighter swipe, animated left to right, on correct answers, solutions and the line currently read aloud.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
